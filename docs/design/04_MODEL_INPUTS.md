# Model-input inventory

This is a design/data acquisition map, not a list of independent knobs. Proposed
inputs remain unresolved until appraised and represented in the evidence registry.
Use it with the [boundary](01_PROBLEM_BOUNDARY.md), [loops](02_CAUSAL_LOOPS.md),
and [externalities](03_EXTERNALITIES.md).

## Separate input roles

| Role | What belongs here | What must not happen |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Initial conditions | Population, inventories, installed capacity, land allocation, available cash, enrolled people, and relevant past commitments at the start date. | Initialize every stock at a convenient equilibrium or zero without evidence. |
| Structural relationships | Conservation identities, competing risks, behavioral rules, functional forms, thresholds, and accounting boundaries. | Hide a causal premise inside a coefficient or treat correlations as equations automatically. |
| Parameters and uncertainty | Response functions, hazards, unit costs, durations, elasticities, and uncertainty/covariance appropriate to each definition. | Use a point estimate or independence assumption merely because it is easier to sample. |
| Exogenous paths | Specified weather, macro conditions, external prices, or fixed treatment/policy paths for this experiment. | Supply outcomes externally while claiming their feedback is generated by the model. |
| Levers and decision rules | Feasible practice, price, procurement, access, enrollment, or funding actions; signals available to the actor at each decision time. | Give actors future information, unlimited finance, instantaneous capacity, or global optimization by default. |
| Observation processes | Survey/sensor/claims definitions, selection, coverage, error, time alignment, reporting lag, and missingness. | Equate latent earlier risk with a convenient measured threshold or purchases with intake. |
| Validation data | Separate historical/reference trajectories and holdouts; behavior under edge conditions. | Calibrate on the holdout or label reproduced inputs as independent validation. |
| Endogenous outputs | Prices, premiums, adoption, margins, disease trajectories, utilization, realized savings, and value distribution when included in the boundary. | Enter the desired result as both an assumption and a model output. |

## Record required before a number enters the engine

Each input specification must identify its stable key and package/loop IDs; role;
definition, units, population, geography, and period; source artifact and immutable
hash; transformation; status and evidence grade; uncertainty and dependencies;
valid range and extrapolation policy; intended equation; and validation test.
For a response function, retain shape/structural uncertainty as well as coefficient
uncertainty. Record what null or opposing evidence would change the specification.

Keep raw sources under the [data policy](../../data/README.md), derived inputs in
reproducible transforms, empirical parameters in `evidence/parameters.yaml`, and
scenario choices in scenario definitions. Restricted transaction or clinical data
must not be committed; use permitted aggregates, fixtures, and provenance.

No new numerical values are proposed here. An unresolved input blocks a substantive
result that depends on it. A labeled synthetic experiment may test software
mechanics, but it must not populate a business case or establish a clinical effect.

## Acquisition and implementation order

The proposed health evidence acquisition contracts are
[RFC-57](../rfcs/RFC-57-longitudinal-identification.md) for repeated-state
progression/reversal and [RFC-58](../rfcs/RFC-58-food-pathway-identification.md) for
a defined dietary contrast. They feed I-01/I-07 and P-02 without activating the
later economic feedbacks or treating menu labels as clinical parameters.

1. Finish I-01 and the applicable health evidence/definition work in I-07 for the
   existing v0.1 acceptance criteria. A separate PreChronic cohort remains later
   work; do not insert it merely to satisfy the broader scenario narrative.
2. After that gate, select one product/region and gather matched I-02–I-06/I-09/I-11
   evidence for P-01 and a small set of interacting supply/adoption loops. Avoid
   assembling an inventory of unrelated datasets before choosing a decision.
3. Define the earlier-risk population and intervention, then assemble I-07–I-09
   evidence for P-02 and a chosen funding response. Test a health/cost pathway
   before claiming a closed prevention-finance loop.
4. Add material I-10 spillovers to that boundary and evaluate both the physical
   effect and the possible payment mechanism. Add further complexity only when
   it changes a decision or resolves an identified validation failure.

Use I-11 and I-12 throughout. Implementation timing remains governed by the
canonical phases, not by the numbering of these design packages.
